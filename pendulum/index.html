Risk is psychological

Risk is not just volatility. It changes with investor behavior, confidence, leverage, and expectations.

Extremes create fragility

When everyone feels safe, risk is usually underpriced. When fear dominates, opportunity quietly grows.

Position matters more than timing

The pendulum is not about calling tops or bottoms — it’s about knowing which side you’re on.

How to use it

1. Assess sentiment

Think about narratives, leverage, valuations, and how confident investors feel.

2. Place the pendulum

You don’t need precision. Rough positioning is enough to reveal risk asymmetry.

3. Adjust behavior

Extreme optimism suggests caution. Extreme pessimism suggests patience and preparation.
