# Plan

High-level roadmap for RiskMeter. Focus on outcomes, not task lists.

## 2026-01-26

- Ship the first new roadmap tool (Drawdown Recovery Scenarios) with EN/ZH parity, polished UX, and reliable persistence.
- Activate SEO infrastructure in production: JSON-LD across page types, sitemap/robots, and canonical + hreflang consistency.
- Strengthen the learning journey with tighter cross-links and CTAs between tools, glossary, and guides to reduce dead ends.
- Establish an ongoing bilingual governance cadence with lightweight QA checks and scheduled content refreshes.
- Validate a neutral sustainability path through a transparent supporter/education offering and a public ethics/guardrails page.

## Vision

RiskMeter should be a trusted reference for investment risk, drawdowns, and behavioral cycles, supported by simple, intuitive tools.

## Guardrails

- Not a trading platform, signal service, or prediction engine.
- Evergreen utility and clarity come before growth and complexity.
