# Plan

High-level roadmap for RiskMeter. Focus on outcomes, not task lists.

## 2026-01-25

- Establish a flagship rebalancing decision flow that feels complete, trustworthy, and consistent across EN/ZH.
- Make the risk glossary the knowledge spine that links definitions, explainers, and tool entry points.
- Shape a coherent behavioral-risk learning journey (drawdowns -> cycles -> rebalancing) that boosts depth and return visits.
- Deliver a fast, mobile-first experience with unified interaction patterns across every tool page.
- Add lightweight signals and feedback capture to guide which tools and content deserve deeper investment next.

## Vision

RiskMeter should be a trusted reference for investment risk, drawdowns, and behavioral cycles, supported by simple, intuitive tools.

## Guardrails

- Not a trading platform, signal service, or prediction engine.
- Evergreen utility and clarity come before growth and complexity.
