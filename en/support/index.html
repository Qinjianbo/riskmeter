Support & Education

Use this page to set expectations, understand local-only data behavior, and share feedback that helps improve the tools.

What to expect

  • Educational tools to reason about drawdowns, behavior, and process.
  • Simple math models designed to inform decisions, not predict returns.
  • No account required; results are meant to support your own plan.

Local-only data

  • Inputs and feedback signals are stored in your browser (localStorage).
  • Clearing browser data or switching devices resets saved values.
  • Nothing is sent to RiskMeter unless you choose to share it.

Expectations & limitations

  • Outputs ignore cash flows, fees, taxes, and path-dependent volatility.
  • RiskMeter is not investment advice and does not recommend securities.
  • Use the tools as guardrails, then validate with your own assumptions.

Feedback channel

  • Use the “Helpful / Needs work” buttons on each tool to save signals locally.
  • Click “Copy feedback summary” and paste it into your preferred channel.
  • Include the page name and what you were trying to accomplish.
