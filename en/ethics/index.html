Ethics & Guardrails

RiskMeter is an educational project. These guardrails describe how we stay neutral, sustainable, and aligned with long-term investors.

Home About / Disclaimer Risk Glossary

1) What we do

  • Provide tools and explanations to help investors reason about risk.
  • Focus on drawdowns, behavior, and process — not prediction.
  • Keep the core toolkit free and available without an account.

2) What we will NOT do

  • No buy/sell recommendations, signals, or price targets.
  • No hidden incentives to push specific assets or products.
  • No performance promises or “guaranteed recovery” language.

3) Sustainability & supporter approach

  • Supporters help fund maintenance, translations, and new tools.
  • Supporter perks, if any, will stay educational (not signals or trades).
  • If sponsorships exist, they will be clearly labeled and non-intrusive.

4) Data & privacy

  • Most interactions are local only (browser storage, no account required).
  • We avoid collecting personal data unless explicitly needed and disclosed.
  • Any future analytics changes will be documented on this page.

5) Feedback & accountability

  • We welcome feedback on clarity, bias, or missing context.
  • Guardrails are reviewed regularly and may evolve with community needs.
